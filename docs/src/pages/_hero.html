Checkpoint-and-gate building

Ratchet Loop: make your AI coding agent prove its work

Your agent says it works. Ratchet makes it show you.

Ratchet Loop plugs into the coding agent you already use. Describe a feature in a sentence. Your agent builds it in small steps, and each step has to pass its tests, match the design and survive a review by a different AI before the next one starts. You approve the plan, walk away, and come back to working, reviewed commits.
