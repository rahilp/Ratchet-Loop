04The viewer

Every run gets its own page. Watch it happen, step by step.

Each run writes a viewer: one web page with the plan, every planned test, every review round and every commit, kept up to date as the agent works. You don't need it, because everything also shows up in your chat. It's there when you want the whole picture at once.

.ratchet/runs/leave-approvals/viewer.html

Example run The real viewer, showing the leave approvals example used on this page.

What's on it

A progress bar with one segment per step. Each step's four gates as chips: Tests, Look and feel, Second opinion, Your OK. What "done" means, the planned tests by ID, who reviewed each round, and the evidence and commit for every finished step.

How to open it

Say Give me the viewer link, and open it if you can. It's a single file in your project at .ratchet/runs/<run>/viewer.html. When Ratchet's small server is running, it refreshes on its own every few seconds and shows "live".

More than one tab

Activity is a time-stamped feed of the run. Learnings shows your saved lessons. New screens link to the clickable prototype, and spec-driven runs show which scenarios are proven so far.

Can I share it with my team?

Yes, on your own network. Ask the agent to serve the viewer for others to see. It gives you a link with a private token in it, and requests without that token are refused. The page follows light or dark mode, and has its own theme switch.
