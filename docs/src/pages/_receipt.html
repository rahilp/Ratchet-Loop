A summary for your pull request.

Someone reviewing an agent's pull request can't tell what was actually checked. So when you approve the feature, the agent writes a receipt that shows it. Paste it into your pull request.

  • Each step, its commit and how long it took
  • Who reviewed each step, and how many rounds
  • Findings you accepted, and why
  • Small issues left for later
  • Time includes time spent waiting on you
