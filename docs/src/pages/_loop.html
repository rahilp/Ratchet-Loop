What happens on every step

The agent writes the tests first and makes sure they fail. Then it writes the code until they pass. The screen is compared with the design, and a reviewer from another AI goes looking for problems. Anything it finds gets fixed and checked again. Once nothing serious is left, the step becomes a commit and the next one starts.

Press play to watch a run, or click a stage to see who does it.
