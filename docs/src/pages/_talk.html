12Talking to it

No commands. Just ask.

Say any of these at any time, in your own words.

  • Where are we?A short status of the run.
  • Pause the build, and resume it when I say so.Nothing is lost. Say "carry on" later, even in a new chat.
  • Cancel this run.The agent asks you to confirm first.
  • Only check in with me at the end.Instead of after every step.
  • Give me the viewer link.A page with the plan, the tests and every review.
  • Which reviewers are available on this machine?Lists the AI tools Ratchet found, and which ones work.
  • Check that Ratchet's hooks are installed for this CLI.It installs them if they are missing.
  • Create a DESIGN.md for this app.Writes the design file from your current screens. Nothing gets built.

{{ICON_ASK}}Try it

Does this count as a yes?

When Ratchet asks for your approval, only a clear yes counts. Type a reply to see how Ratchet reads it.

  • Counts: yes yes, go ahead go ahead approved
  • Capital letters and a full stop or ! at the end are fine.
  • "ok", "sure", 👍 and "yes but…" do not count.
  • Only your first reply counts, and it only answers that one question.

Accept a finding yourself

accept: your reason

If a reviewer blocks on something you think is fine, you can accept it with a reason. The reason is saved in the step's record and in .ratchet/learnings.md. If the code changes later, it gets reviewed again.

Give a step one more round

raise: your reason

When a step hits its review limit, you can give it one more round. This works once per step. Your reason is saved so later reviewers see it.
